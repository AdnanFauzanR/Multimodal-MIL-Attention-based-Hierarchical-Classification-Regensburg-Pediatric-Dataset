Experiment 1: published references and matched local controls

Literature rows have different cohorts/splits and ten initializations for Marcinkevics, versus one local seed. They are not matched replications. No ranking or significance claim is made.

Scores are fractions (0–1). F1 is positive-class binary F1; macro-F1 is a separate column and is the checkpoint monitor. AP and trapezoidal PR area are both reported; the paper AUPR implementation is not assumed to be identical.

Paper severity = complicated versus uncomplicated OR no appendicitis (all patients). Direct q on true appendicitis is reported separately. Original hierarchy and direct marginalization share ranking scores; identical AUROC/AP is expected, and diagnosis decisions are identical.

Softmax binary tables use marginal probabilities at threshold 0.5. Original argmax-derived binary decisions are exported separately. Retained runs are the most recent completed matching runs, not the highest test scores. Untested/missing models remain pending.
